# Project Shield Futures

> **Protecting Minds, Building Futures – A Gun-Free Tomorrow**

A modern, elegant, and minimalist awareness website against gun violence, designed for a 5-6 minute presentation.

## 📁 Project Structure

```
Final-Task/
├── index.html          # Main website file
└── README.md           # This file
```

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Open the website**: Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
2. **No server required**: The site works as a static file - just double-click to open.
3. **For presentation**: Use the scroll wheel or arrow keys to navigate through sections.

## 📝 Presentation Script (English)

### Person A (0:00 - 3:00)

**Introduction & Choice of Side (0:00 - 0:45)**
> "Hello everyone. Today, as anti-gun activists and youth advocates, [Person B's Name] and I are proud to present our digital awareness project called 'Project Shield Futures'. Our main goal is to tackle one specific and essential aspect of gun violence: safety in school communities and youth spaces. We strongly believe that the most effective way to reduce violence is to start where the future is being built—in our schools and neighborhoods."

**Presentation of the Website Concept (0:45 - 1:45)**
> "To reach our audience effectively, we designed a modern, interactive single-page website. Why a website? Because today's youth and young adults receive most of their information through digital screens. A website allows us to combine message clarity, interactive elements, and shareable resources all in one place. As you can see on the screen, the layout is clean, modern, and accessible on mobile phones as well as laptops."

**Focus Area & Core Pillars (1:45 - 3:00)**
> "Our project tackles three main pillars: First, Mental Health & Early Prevention—we want schools to invest in counseling rather than militarization. Second, Universal Background Checks—advocating for stricter community checks before weapon purchases. And third, Gun-Free School Zones—raising awareness among parents and local policymakers. Now, I will pass the microphone to [Person B's Name], who will explain our creative choices, our slogans, and how we plan to deploy this site to make a real impact."

### Person B (3:00 - 6:00)

**Creative Strategy, Slogans & Visual Choices (3:00 - 4:15)**
> "Thank you, [Person A's Name]. When designing this project, we strictly respected all safety guidelines: zero weapons, zero violence, and zero blood are depicted on our platform. Instead of using fear or graphic images, we decided to use positive reinforcement and hope. Our main slogan is: 'Books, Not Bullets. Dreams, Not Drama.' We chose a vibrant emerald green color palette to symbolize growth, life, and safety. The shield logo represents protection and community solidarity rather than defense through weapons."

**Deployment & Strategy to Convince (4:15 - 5:30)**
> "Now, where and how will we use this project to convince people? Social Media Campaigns: We will promote short video clips on TikTok and Instagram targeting 15-25 year olds, directing them directly to this landing page. School Posters with QR Codes: We plan to display minimal, eye-catching posters on high school and university campuses. Students can scan the QR code to sign our online 'Student Pledge'. Local Policy Engagement: The petition section allows citizens to directly send an automated email to their local representatives advocating for safer community laws."

**Conclusion (5:30 - 6:00)**
> "In conclusion, 'Shield Futures' proves that we can raise awareness effectively through peace, education, and modern design without resorting to violent imagery. Thank you for your attention, and we are ready for your questions!"

## 💡 Presentation Tips

- **Timing**: Practice with a timer to ensure each person speaks for approximately 2 minutes 50 seconds
- **Mouse Control**: Person A controls the mouse for the first 3 minutes, then Person B takes over
- **Scrolling**: Scroll to each section as you introduce it
- **Eye Contact**: Maintain eye contact with the audience, not just the screen
- **Confidence**: Speak clearly and with conviction

## 🔒 Safety & Compliance

✅ **NO weapons** - No images, drawings, or references to firearms
✅ **NO violence** - No blood, no aggressive imagery
✅ **NO fear-based messaging** - Only positive, hopeful content
✅ **School-focused** - Emphasis on education and youth protection
✅ **Peaceful design** - Calm colors, protective symbols, growth imagery
